GTA Online: Best Businesses to Own Right Now

Leonida Report Staff
GuidesGTA Online

GTA Online has added dozens of business types over the years, and not all of them are worth the setup cost. Here's a practical breakdown of what actually earns its keep.

Nightclub

The Nightclub remains one of the best value purchases in GTA Online because it passively generates goods from other owned businesses you might already have running, effectively letting you collect multiple income streams from one central location. It's consistently recommended as an early priority purchase.

Acid Lab

Relatively cheap to buy and upgrade compared to older businesses, with a strong profit margin per in-game day if you keep supplies stocked and sell consistently. A strong pick for players without a huge starting budget.

Salvage Yard and specialty businesses

Newer specialty businesses tied to updates often come with strong short-term earning potential tied to specific mission chains. These are worth prioritizing when they're current, since Rockstar tends to shift player attention toward whatever business type headlines the latest update.

What to avoid

Some of the original 2016-era businesses, certain standalone production businesses without the Nightclub's consolidation bonus, have a much longer payback period relative to their setup cost. Unless you're specifically chasing a themed playthrough, they're a weaker use of in-game cash than newer options.

GTA Online's economy shifts with every content update, so treat specific numbers as a snapshot. Always check current setup and upgrade costs in-game before committing a big chunk of cash to any business.
