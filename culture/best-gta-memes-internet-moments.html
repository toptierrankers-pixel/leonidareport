The Best GTA Memes and Internet Moments of All Time

Leonida Report Staff
CultureSan AndreasGTA VGTA 6

Few franchises have generated as much internet culture as Grand Theft Auto. From glitches to voice lines to fan-made chaos, here are some of the moments that outlived the games they came from.

"Ah shit, here we go again"

CJ's San Andreas line became one of the most quoted phrases in gaming, endlessly remixed and reused as a reaction to any repeating bad situation, well beyond gaming spaces entirely.

Big Smoke's order

A single fast-food order recited by San Andreas's Big Smoke turned into one of the most enduring gaming memes ever produced, remixed into music, video edits, and reaction content for two decades and counting.

GTA V's flying car and other physics chaos

GTA V's chaotic ragdoll physics and cheat codes produced an entire genre of clip compilations, players intentionally breaking the game's physics for pure comedic chaos, that helped fuel its early YouTube and Twitch popularity.

Trevor Philips as a meme unto himself

Trevor's volatility made him an instant source of quotable, clippable chaos, and his unpredictable reactions to mundane situations became a recurring meme template well beyond the game itself.

The GTA 6 trailer leak and reveal

The forced-early leak ahead of GTA 6's first trailer became its own internet moment, with Rockstar's response, releasing the trailer early rather than letting the leak dominate, becoming a case study people still reference in discussions about handling leaks gracefully.

Why GTA memes have such staying power

GTA's combination of dark humor, chaotic physics, and quotable writing gives fans an unusually rich well of material to draw from. Few franchises have produced this much recurring internet culture across so many separate games.
