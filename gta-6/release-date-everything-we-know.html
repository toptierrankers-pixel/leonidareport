GTA 6 Release Date: Everything We Know So Far

Leonida Report Staff

Grand Theft Auto 6 is the most anticipated video game release in years, and the release date has been a moving target since Rockstar first confirmed the game was in development. Here is what has actually been confirmed, straight from Rockstar and Take-Two, rather than what rumor threads want to be true.

The confirmed timeline

Rockstar broke its usual silence in early 2022 when it confirmed active development on the next Grand Theft Auto for the first time. The first trailer landed in December 2023 after an early leak forced Rockstar's hand, and it immediately became one of the most-watched game trailers ever released.

Take-Two initially guided investors toward a fall 2025 launch window. That date slipped, and Rockstar pushed the release into 2026 to give the game more polish time, a pattern consistent with how the studio has handled every mainline GTA release since GTA IV.

Release dates for GTA 6 have shifted more than once already. Treat any specific date, including the ones discussed here, as subject to change until Rockstar posts it directly on their own channels.

Platforms

GTA 6 has been confirmed for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S. Rockstar has not committed to a PC release date alongside the console launch, which matches the pattern from GTA V, where the PC version arrived well over a year after consoles. Expect the same staggered approach here.

Price

Take-Two executives have hinted in earnings calls that GTA 6 could carry a higher price point than the traditional sixty or seventy dollar standard, given the scale of the project and rising development costs across the industry. Nothing is official yet, but a premium price tag above prior GTA entries would not be surprising.

What could still change

  • The exact release date, which Rockstar has already adjusted once
  • Whether a PC version launches day one or later
  • Final pricing and whether special or collector's editions are offered
  • Pre-order bonuses and early access windows

We'll keep this page updated as Rockstar shares more. Bookmark it and check the GTA 6 hub for the latest breakdowns.
