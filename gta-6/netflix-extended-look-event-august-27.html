GTA 6's Extended Look Event Is Coming to Netflix on August 27

Leonida Report Staff
GTA 6NewsTrailers

Grand Theft Auto 6's next big moment isn't landing on YouTube or a Rockstar livestream, it's coming to Netflix. An extended look event is set to stream on Netflix on August 27, giving fans their most detailed look yet at Leonida ahead of launch.

What is the extended look event?

Rather than a short teaser drop, an "extended look" typically means a longer, more substantial piece of footage than a standard trailer, closer to an extended gameplay showcase than a cinematic tease. Rockstar has used extended-format reveals before to go deeper on mechanics, world detail, and story setup once the initial hype-building teaser has already done its job. Landing this one on Netflix rather than a standard game trailer drop is a notable shift in how this event is being distributed.

Why Netflix?

Netflix has been steadily expanding beyond its core streaming library into games and live interactive events over the past few years. Hosting a GTA 6 extended look is a significant crossover moment, putting one of gaming's most anticipated releases in front of Netflix's massive, largely non-gaming subscriber base, and it signals just how mainstream GTA 6's launch is expected to be.

What to expect

While Rockstar hasn't published a detailed shot list ahead of time, extended look events like this one are typically used to show deeper environmental detail across Leonida, more of Jason and Lucia's story and dynamic, and potentially first looks at gameplay systems that haven't been shown in the trailers so far. Expect this to be the most substantial GTA 6 media drop since the second trailer.

How to watch

The event is set to stream on Netflix on August 27. As with any major GTA reveal, expect it to be clipped, broken down, and reacted to across every gaming platform within minutes of going live, so even without a Netflix subscription, coverage will be everywhere almost immediately.

This is a developing story. Details on exact timing and format may shift as Netflix and Rockstar share more ahead of the 27th, check back here for updates.

Pre-order while you wait

With the extended look event closing in, now's a good time to lock in your copy if you haven't already.
