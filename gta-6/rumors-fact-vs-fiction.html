GTA 6 Rumors: Separating Fact From Fan Theory

Leonida Report Staff
GTA 6Rumors

Few games generate as much rumor traffic as Grand Theft Auto. With GTA 6, years of leaks, datamines, and speculation have blurred the line between confirmed detail and wishful thinking. Here's a level-headed pass through the biggest rumors.

Rumor: the map is the largest in GTA history

Likely true, unconfirmed in exact numbers. Trailer footage and prior leaked build data both point toward a map larger and more varied than GTA V's Los Santos and Blaine County. Rockstar has not published official size figures, and any specific square mileage circulating online should be treated as fan estimation.

Rumor: multiplayer will be built in from day one

Plausible. Given how central GTA Online became to Take-Two's revenue after GTA V, it would be a major strategic shift for Rockstar to launch GTA 6 without an online component ready at or near launch. Nothing officially detailed has been shared about its structure yet.

Rumor: the game will run on a subscription-style live service model

Unconfirmed and likely overstated. Rockstar's business model for GTA Online has always been built on one-time purchase plus microtransactions rather than subscriptions. There is no credible indication that changes for GTA 6's core game.

Rumor: a full character roster leaked

Treat with heavy skepticism. Character roster leaks have circulated for years across GTA forums, most sourced from unverifiable claims or misattributed casting-call documents. Only Jason and Lucia have been officially confirmed by Rockstar as of the trailers released so far.

The safest rule with any GTA leak: if it did not come from Rockstar's own channels or a credible outlet citing an on-record source, treat it as unconfirmed no matter how detailed it sounds.

Rumor: the release will slip again

Historically likely. Rockstar has delayed nearly every mainline GTA release at least once, and GTA 6's date has already shifted from initial guidance. A further adjustment would not break precedent.
