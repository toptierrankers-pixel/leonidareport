GTA 6 vs GTA 5: What's Actually Changing

Leonida Report Staff
GTA 6GTA VComparison

GTA V set a bar few games have ever reached, and GTA 6 has to follow it. Here's a direct look at what's actually changing between the two, based on what Rockstar has confirmed rather than what fans hope for.

Setting

GTA V put players in Los Santos and Blaine County, a West Coast pastiche. GTA 6 moves to Leonida, Rockstar's fictional take on Florida, and brings back Vice City as its centerpiece. Trailer footage suggests more environmental variety, swamps, suburbs, and multiple urban centers, than GTA V's single-metro structure offered.

Protagonists

GTA V split its story across three unconnected criminals, Michael, Franklin, and Trevor, who crossed paths occasionally. GTA 6 narrows that down to two leads, Jason and Lucia, whose story is framed as personal and romantic rather than a loose crew of strangers. It's a tighter structure by design, and it also marks the series' first playable female protagonist.

Technology

GTA V launched on PS3 and Xbox 360 before later console generations caught up to it. GTA 6 is built natively for PS5 and Xbox Series X/S from day one, meaning lighting, water, crowd density, and physics simulation are working with a full console generation of extra headroom compared to what GTA V had at launch.

Tone

GTA V's satire targeted 2013-era Los Angeles culture: reality TV, self-help grifters, and social media's early influence. GTA 6's marketing leans into a more current version of that same instinct, viral internet culture, influencer economies, and modern Florida's particular chaos, updated for a decade of change in how people actually behave online.

What's staying the same

The core GTA formula, open-world chaos, mission-based crime storytelling, and Rockstar's signature dark humor, hasn't gone anywhere based on what's been shown. GTA 6 looks like an evolution of the formula GTA V perfected, not a reinvention of it.

Waiting on GTA 6? There's no better time to finally play (or replay) GTA V.
