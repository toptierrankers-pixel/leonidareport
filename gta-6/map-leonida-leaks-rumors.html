GTA 6 Map: Leonida, Vice City, and What Leaks Suggest

Leonida Report Staff
GTA 6MapLeonidaVice City

The GTA 6 setting is the biggest departure from expectations since Rockstar first teased a return to the sun-and-neon world of Vice City. Instead of simply calling the map Vice City again, Rockstar built an entire fictional state around it: Leonida, a clear stand-in for modern Florida.

Leonida, Rockstar's take on Florida

Leonida is Rockstar's fictional version of the Sunshine State, and the choice makes sense given how much real-world Florida culture, from viral news stories to its swamps and strip malls, has fed internet culture for the past decade. Vice City sits at the heart of the state, rebuilt from the ground up rather than reused from prior games.

What the trailers have shown

Across the released trailers, Rockstar has shown swampland, suburban sprawl, downtown high-rises, beaches, and rural backroads, suggesting a map with far more environmental variety than GTA V's Los Santos and Blaine County. Leaked assets and modding community datamines from earlier in development pointed to a map larger than any prior GTA world, though Rockstar has never confirmed exact numbers.

Multiple cities, not just Vice City

Footage suggests more than one major urban center may be explorable, echoing San Andreas's three-city structure rather than GTA V's single metro area. Whether every area is available from the start of the game or unlocked progressively (as GTA V handled Los Santos and Blaine County) has not been confirmed.

A word of caution: map leaks from years-old builds circulate constantly and often show cut or placeholder content. Treat unofficial map images as a snapshot of a point in development, not the finished product.

Why this setting matters

Vice City has always been fan-favorite territory since the 2002 original, and building an entire modern state around it gives Rockstar room to do things the original 1980s-set Vice City never could: modern social media culture, a contemporary economy, and a Florida-man tone that fits GTA's satire perfectly.
