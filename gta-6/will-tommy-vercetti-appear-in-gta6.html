Will Tommy Vercetti Appear in GTA 6? Every Possibility, Ranked

Leonida Report Staff
GTA 6Vice CityRumorsCharacters

Part of our GTA 6: Everything We Know So Far hub.

Every time Rockstar shows a new piece of Leonida, the same question ripples through the fanbase: is Tommy Vercetti in this game? Nobody at Rockstar has confirmed or denied anything, so this is pure speculation, but it's speculation worth taking seriously, because Vice City's shadow is impossible to avoid with GTA 6 returning to the same city. Here's every version of "Tommy shows up" that fans are floating, and which one actually fits how Rockstar tends to handle its own history.

What we actually know: nothing, officially

To be completely clear before we get into theory-crafting: Rockstar has not confirmed Tommy Vercetti in any capacity for GTA 6. No trailer has shown him, no leak has meaningfully confirmed him, and Rockstar has historically stayed quiet about legacy characters until a game is out. Everything below is fan speculation, including ours.

Possibility one: a background mention, nothing more

This is the safest and, honestly, most likely outcome. Rockstar has a long history of nodding to its own continuity through background details rather than direct appearances, a radio mention, a news headline, a building named after him, graffiti referencing the Vercetti era. GTA V did exactly this with CJ and San Andreas, dropping in references without ever bringing the character back on screen. A Tommy easter egg would satisfy longtime fans without forcing the new story to bend around an old one.

Possibility two: older, still running things

The more dramatic fan theory: Tommy survived, aged into his sixties or seventies, and is still a name that matters in Vice City's underworld, whether as a retired kingpin, a legend people still invoke, or a background power player Jason and Lucia's story occasionally brushes up against. This is the version fans want most, since it means an actual on-screen appearance, but it's also the riskiest for Rockstar's story. Vice City's setting has moved on decades since the original game, and giving Tommy an active role risks overshadowing two new protagonists Rockstar has spent years building hype around.

Possibility three: dead, and only a legend now

The quieter, more narratively elegant option: Tommy is gone, and what's left is reputation, stories about the Vercetti crime family's rise and fall in the 1980s, told secondhand by other characters the way real cities mythologize their old crime bosses. This lets Rockstar honor Vice City's history without needing to actually write Tommy as a character again, and it fits the more grounded, consequence-driven tone the extended look event has been hinting at. Leonida gets its own local legend, and longtime fans get the closure of knowing what happened to him, even if it's just through rumor and reputation in-world.

So, which one is it?

If we had to bet: some version of possibility one or three. Rockstar tends to treat its old protagonists as history rather than active players in a new story, and Jason and Lucia's Bonnie and Clyde dynamic is clearly meant to be the emotional center of GTA 6, not shared screen time with a returning legend. A legacy nod, a mention, a piece of Vercetti-era graffiti, maybe a mission reference to the old family, feels far more likely than Tommy walking on screen. But we've been wrong about Rockstar before, and honestly, we'd love to be wrong about this one too.

This entire post is speculation. Nothing here is confirmed by Rockstar. We'll update if that ever changes.

However Tommy's story wraps up, Vice City's original chapter is still worth playing. Pre-order GTA 6 while you wait to find out.
