Rockstar Games

The Complete History of Rockstar Games

Leonida Report Staff

Rockstar Games was founded in 1998 as a publishing label under Take-Two Interactive, built around a small group of developers who had already been working on an ambitious top-down crime game. That project became the original Grand Theft Auto, and it set the tone for everything Rockstar would become.

Building an identity

Rockstar's early identity was built on controversy as much as gameplay. Games like GTA and later Manhunt drew heavy media criticism and political attention, which, whether intentional or not, cemented Rockstar's reputation as the studio willing to push further than anyone else in mainstream gaming.

The 3D era changes everything

GTA III's 2001 release was the turning point. It proved open-world, physics-driven, mission-based crime games could work in full 3D, and its commercial success gave Rockstar the resources and creative freedom to expand aggressively. Vice City and San Andreas followed in rapid succession, each one bigger and more ambitious than the last.

Beyond GTA

Rockstar built out multiple internal studios during this period, expanding beyond GTA into Max Payne, Bully, Midnight Club, and the first Red Dead Revolver. This diversification proved Rockstar wasn't a one-franchise studio, even as GTA remained the clear centerpiece.

The HD era and total dominance

GTA IV in 2008 marked Rockstar's full transition to a more cinematic, technically ambitious approach. Red Dead Redemption followed in 2010 and became a critical and commercial landmark in its own right. Then GTA V arrived in 2013 and became one of the best-selling entertainment products in history, a status it still holds more than a decade later thanks to GTA Online's continued growth.

Red Dead Redemption 2 and the modern era

2018's Red Dead Redemption 2 pushed Rockstar's reputation for detail and world-building to new extremes, and is widely considered one of the greatest games ever made. It also drew renewed scrutiny over Rockstar's demanding development culture and crunch practices, a conversation the studio has had to publicly navigate since.

Where Rockstar stands now

Today, Rockstar Games operates as a global studio under Take-Two, with GTA Online continuing to generate massive ongoing revenue years after GTA V's original release. All eyes are now on GTA 6, widely expected to be one of the largest entertainment launches in history, and the next chapter in a studio that has defined open-world gaming for over two decades.
