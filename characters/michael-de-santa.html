Michael De Santa: The Retired Criminal Who Couldn't Stay Retired

Leonida Report Staff
CharactersGTA V

Michael De Santa, formerly Michael Townley, is one of Grand Theft Auto V's three protagonists, and the character whose past drives most of the game's central conflict.

Background

Michael faked his own death years earlier as part of a witness protection deal after a heist gone wrong, and has since been living a comfortable but miserable life in Los Santos, surrounded by a family that barely respects him. When his past inevitably resurfaces, Michael is pulled back into exactly the kind of criminal work he thought he'd escaped.

Personality

Michael is equal parts darkly funny and genuinely pathetic, a man who got everything he thought he wanted and found it empty. His scenes with his dysfunctional family provide some of GTA V's sharpest comedy, while his history with Trevor provides its most dramatic tension.

Ned Luke's performance

Ned Luke's voice work leaned fully into Michael's mid-life crisis energy, equal parts self-pity and menace, and became one of the most quoted performances in the game.

Legacy

Michael's storyline, particularly his unraveling relationship with Trevor, is often cited as the emotional backbone of GTA V's three-protagonist structure.
