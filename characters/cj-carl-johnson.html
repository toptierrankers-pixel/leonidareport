Carl "CJ" Johnson: San Andreas's Emotional Core

Leonida Report Staff
CharactersSan Andreas

Carl "CJ" Johnson is the protagonist of Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas, and consistently ranks among the most beloved leads the series has ever produced.

Background

CJ left Los Santos five years before the game begins, escaping the gang life that claimed friends and family around him. His mother's murder pulls him back home, straight into a web of corrupt cops, crumbling gang alliances, and his own family's dysfunction, centered on the Grove Street Families and his complicated relationship with his brother Sweet.

Personality

CJ is more grounded and reflective than most GTA leads before or since. He's loyal almost to a fault, genuinely wants better for the people around him, and carries real guilt over the state he finds Grove Street in when he returns. That emotional throughline gives San Andreas a weight the series hadn't attempted before.

Voice performance

CJ was voiced by Young Maylay, whose performance balanced toughness with real vulnerability, key to making CJ's family drama land as hard as it does.

Legacy

CJ's story, and lines like "Ah shit, here we go again," have outlived the game itself as some of the most quoted moments in gaming. He's frequently cited as the series' best-written protagonist, and San Andreas's emotional reputation rests heavily on his shoulders.
