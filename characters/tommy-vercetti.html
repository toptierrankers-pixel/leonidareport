Tommy Vercetti: The Scarface of Vice City

Leonida Report Staff
CharactersVice City

Tommy Vercetti is the protagonist of Grand Theft Auto: Vice City, and arguably the character most responsible for turning GTA protagonists into genuine movie-caliber leads.

Background

Fresh out of a fifteen-year prison sentence, Tommy is sent by the Forelli crime family to Vice City to run a drug deal that immediately goes wrong, leaving him without his money or his product. What follows is a rise from small-time enforcer to the most powerful crime boss in Vice City, told with an obvious debt to Scarface and Miami Vice.

Personality

Tommy is sharper and more self-aware than the gangster archetype he's built on. He's short-tempered and violent, but also genuinely funny, ambitious, and weirdly principled about loyalty for a man who spends the whole game betraying and being betrayed by nearly everyone around him.

Ray Liotta's performance

Ray Liotta's voice work gave Tommy real star power, a mix of menace and dark humor that suited Vice City's pastel, satirical take on the 1980s perfectly. It remains one of the most celebrated performances in the series.

Legacy

Tommy's rise from street enforcer to empire-builder became the template GTA protagonists followed for years afterward. With GTA 6 returning to Vice City, Tommy's shadow is impossible to avoid, even if Rockstar has made clear Jason and Lucia are telling their own, unconnected story.
