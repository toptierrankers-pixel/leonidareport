Trevor Philips: GTA's Most Unhinged Protagonist

Leonida Report Staff
CharactersGTA V

Trevor Philips is one of Grand Theft Auto V's three protagonists, and by a wide margin the most volatile personality the series has ever put in a player's hands.

Background

Trevor runs a drug and gunrunning operation out of the desert town of Sandy Shores, a former pilot and Michael's old partner in crime who never got the chance to fake his own death and walk away. His reunion with Michael, once he learns the truth about what really happened years earlier, drives much of GTA V's back half.

Personality

Trevor is unpredictable in a way that makes nearly every scene he's in feel genuinely dangerous. He can flip from friendly to murderous in seconds, yet the writing never lets him become a pure cartoon, there's real hurt and betrayal underneath the chaos, particularly in how he processes what Michael did to him.

Steven Ogg's performance

Steven Ogg's voice work is widely considered one of the standout performances in gaming, finding both the dark comedy and the genuine menace in Trevor without ever losing the character's strange, damaged humanity.

Legacy

Trevor is the character most players quote first when talking about GTA V, and his volatility remains a high bar for any future Rockstar protagonist looking to leave a similar impression.
