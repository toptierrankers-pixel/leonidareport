Every GTA Game Ranked From Worst to Best

Leonida Report Staff
RankingsGTA IIIVice CitySan AndreasGTA IVGTA VGTA Online

Grand Theft Auto has been reinventing open-world gaming since 1997. Ranking the full series means weighing ambition against how well each game has aged, and being honest about which entries were genuinely great versus just influential for their time. Here's the list, worst to best.

10. Grand Theft Auto (1997)

The original is historically important and genuinely inventive for its era, but it is a rough, top-down, largely mission-checklist experience by any modern standard. Worth understanding for context, not for actually playing today.

9. Grand Theft Auto 2 (1999)

An iteration on the original with gang reputation systems layered in, but still constrained by the same top-down structure. A minor step forward that gets overshadowed almost immediately by what came next.

8. Grand Theft Auto: Advance (2004)

A surprisingly competent handheld entry, but by nature a stripped-down experience that can't compete with the console mainline games on any meaningful axis beyond portability.

7. Grand Theft Auto IV (2008)

A tonal hard-left turn into gritty realism that alienated some fans expecting more Vice City chaos, but one of the most narratively mature entries in the series with a genuinely great protagonist in Niko Bellic. Ages better with hindsight than it was credited at launch.

6. Grand Theft Auto III (2001)

The game that invented the modern open-world genre as we know it. Rough by today's standards mechanically, but its historical weight is undeniable, and Liberty City still holds up as a mood piece.

5. Grand Theft Auto: Vice City (2002)

Style as substance. Vice City's 1980s pastel-and-neon aesthetic, killer soundtrack, and Tony Montana-inspired story turned it into a cultural landmark that still gets referenced constantly, including in GTA 6's own marketing.

4. Grand Theft Auto Online (2013-ongoing)

Technically an expansion of GTA V, but it has evolved into its own sprawling game over more than a decade of updates, heists, and content drops. Its longevity alone earns it a spot this high.

3. Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas (2004)

The most ambitious GTA before the HD era: three cities, RPG-lite mechanics, and one of the best video game stories of its generation through CJ and Grove Street. Still beloved for good reason.

2. Grand Theft Auto V (2013)

A technical showcase and a genuinely funny, sprawling crime saga across three protagonists. Its staying power, helped enormously by GTA Online, makes it one of the best-selling entertainment products ever made.

1. Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas or GTA V, depending on the day

Honestly, the top spot is a coin flip between ambition (San Andreas) and polish (GTA V), and reasonable fans land on either side. What is not debatable is that both represent Rockstar at its absolute best, and GTA 6 has a genuinely difficult act to follow.
