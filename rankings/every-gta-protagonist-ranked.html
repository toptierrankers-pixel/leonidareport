Every GTA Protagonist Ranked

Leonida Report Staff
RankingsCharactersSan AndreasGTA VGTA IVVice CityGTA 6

GTA protagonists range from mostly silent avatars to some of gaming's most quoted characters. Here's how they stack up.

8. Claude (GTA III)

Completely silent and defined entirely by player projection. Historically important as an experiment, but not a character in any traditional sense.

7. Tommy Vercetti (Vice City)

A great voice performance from Ray Liotta and a strong Scarface-inspired arc, though the character stays fairly one-note by design: ambition and violence.

6. Jason and Lucia (GTA 6)

Too early to fully rank, but the Bonnie and Clyde framing and Lucia's status as the series' first female lead already make this pairing one of the more narratively interesting choices Rockstar has made.

5. Franklin Clinton (GTA V)

A grounded, relatable everyman caught between loyalty and ambition. Consistently the most sympathetic of GTA V's trio.

4. Michael De Santa (GTA V)

A washed-up former criminal dragged back into the life, played with real comedic and dramatic range. His family dysfunction subplot is some of the series' best writing.

3. Niko Bellic (GTA IV)

The most emotionally serious protagonist in the series. Niko's war-veteran backstory and disillusionment with the American Dream gave GTA IV a weight the series had never attempted before.

2. Trevor Philips (GTA V)

Unhinged, unpredictable, and unforgettable. Trevor is the character most players quote, and his volatility makes every scene he's in feel dangerous in a way no other GTA character has matched.

1. Carl "CJ" Johnson (San Andreas)

The emotional backbone of the series' most ambitious story. CJ's return to Grove Street, his complicated family, and his rise through San Andreas's criminal underworld remain the gold standard for GTA storytelling two decades later.
