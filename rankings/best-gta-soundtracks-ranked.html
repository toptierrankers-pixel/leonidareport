Best GTA Soundtracks Ranked

Leonida Report Staff
RankingsVice CitySan AndreasGTA VSoundtrack

GTA's radio stations are as much a character in the series as any protagonist. Here's how the best soundtracks in the franchise stack up.

4. GTA IV

A more subdued, mood-driven soundtrack fitting Liberty City's grittier tone. Less iconic than its predecessors, but the talk radio and satirical ad breaks remain some of the sharpest writing in the series.

3. GTA V

An enormous, genre-spanning lineup with genuinely great original stations, a killer West Coast Classics run, and standout original scoring during heists and chases. Its sheer scale is unmatched.

2. San Andreas

The definitive early-90s West Coast hip hop and G-funk soundtrack, matched perfectly to CJ's story and Los Santos's identity. Radio Los Santos alone is one of gaming's most beloved fictional stations.

1. Vice City

Nothing in gaming captures a decade the way Vice City's soundtrack captures the 1980s. Flock of Seagulls, Michael Jackson, Wang Chung, and a dozen other era-defining tracks turn every drive through the city into a mood. It set the template every GTA soundtrack since has chased.

GTA 6's Vice City return has a soundtrack to live up to. Given Rockstar's licensing budget and the setting, expect a heavy mix of 2020s pop and hip hop alongside deep cuts, in the same spirit as Vice City's 80s curation.
